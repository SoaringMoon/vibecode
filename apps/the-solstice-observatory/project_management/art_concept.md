# Project Vision: The Solstice Observatory

## Concept Overview
"The Solstice Observatory" is a monumental, hand-crafted vector artwork composed entirely in SVG. It depicts a sacred architectural observatory positioned in an otherworldly basalt desert at the moment of an astronomical total solar eclipse.

## Thematic Elements
1. **The Cosmic Sphere**: High-precision celestial armillary rings, coordinate arcs, constellation tracings, and logarithmic spirals representing ancient mathematical astronomy.
2. **The Corona and Eclipse**: A dramatic black obsidian lunar disc occluding the sun, radiating 48 coronal flare beams, Baily's Beads diamond flare, and solar prominence plasma loops.
3. **The Geological Horizon**: Vast undulating basalt dunes and jagged serrated volcanic spires receding into purple-indigo atmospheric haze.
4. **The Monolithic Sanctuary**: Massive stepped brutalist pylons, a colossal central circular aperture framing the horizon, an astrolabe dial, and a grand 36-tier ceremonial staircase descending into the sands.
5. **The Mirrored Waters**: A perfectly calm rectangular obsidian reflecting pool mirroring the coronal light and ripples of cosmic alignment.
6. **The Solitary Witness**: A robed astronomer figure standing on a foreground promontory holding an armillary staff, observing the cosmic convergence.

## Dimensional Standards
- **Aspect Ratio**: 3:4 (Portrait)
- **Native SVG Canvas Coordinates**: `viewBox="0 0 2400 3200"`
- **Native Dimensions**: 2400 px width × 3200 px height
- **Export Constraint Verification**: Smallest side is 2400 px (strictly $\ge 2000$ px minimum requirement)
- **Ultra-HD Mode**: 3600 × 4800 px (1.5x) and 4800 × 6400 px (2x)
