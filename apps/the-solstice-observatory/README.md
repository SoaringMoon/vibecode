# The Solstice Observatory
An app displaying an attempt at creating an art masterpiece.

# Prompt

You will be building an app that displays and exports a single SVG artwork that you create.
You will be iteratively refining your masterpiece.

What this application will not be: 
- It will not be a forge, generator, or AI image-model based generation tool for automatic asset creation.
- It should not have interactive animations of any kind. You will be exporting a single static PNG image. Static images are not interactive.

Specifications:
- You will need to export your artwork in PNG format with the smallest side of your aspect ratio being at least 2000px.
- You will be "hard coding" art, in the SVG format.
- Make sure that the elements are exported with the amount of aliasing you intend if any.
- Application features only need to cover display of the art, and export of the PNG image according to specifications.

Considerations:
- Your art does not need to be technically impressive to be interesting.
- Your art is not limited by palette, shapes, or concepts. Only through clever utility of what SVG code can be used to create.
- Your art may be as complex or simple as you desire.
- Your art may use as much or little of the canvas as you like.
- Instead of a single monolithic SVG file, consider using layer of SVG to compose your full piece. (To make editing easier.)
- The `project_management` directory serves as only a long-term memory for you. It doesn't even need to be human readable. Feel free to put your thoughts there.
- Make sure that placement of elements, layer hierarchy, z-depth, etc; are displaying as you intend them to.

---

The only other direction you will be given, after the application is exporting without errors is: "Continue iterating upon and refining your masterpiece."

Continue iterating upon and refining your masterpiece.
Refinement doesn't always mean adding new elements.
Sometimes refinement means improving existing elements.
